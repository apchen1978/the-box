The Box

A manufacturer files this under cost. The customer might be filing it under product.

Demo thought experiment · possibility, not evidence
↑
Robot · $39.99 The other product

You bought a robot. It came in a box.

The box wasn't just packaging.

You designed it as a cost. What if the customer experiences it as part of the product?

Demo commercial thought experiment. It is not evidence that customers keep packaging, that it creates revenue, that it reduces waste, or that it improves retention. Possibility ≠ evidence.
One verified fact: the cardboard box was inducted into the National Toy Hall of Fame in 2005 — The Strong National Museum of Play.
